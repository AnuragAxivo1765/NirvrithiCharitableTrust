How It Works

Borrowing Equipment

All equipment is provided free of charge on a loan basis to palliative care patients and their families. Availability shown above reflects current stock. Please contact us to arrange collection or delivery.

1. Contact Us

Get in touch with Pookoya Thangal Hospice to discuss your equipment needs and confirm availability.

2. Equipment Arranged

We will coordinate the handover of the required equipment. Available for home collection or delivery where possible.

3. Use at Home

Equipment is provided at no cost for the duration of care. Please return it in good condition when no longer needed.
